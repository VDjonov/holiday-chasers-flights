About Holiday Chasers

Holiday Chasers is a fare-finding and trip-planning site for travellers anywhere in Ireland.

Cork and Dublin are the two biggest airports, and they are the two airports the site searches from. The deal board shows return fares for weekend breaks and one-week trips. A trip guide can add hotels, car hire and a rough cost of the days away, so you can see the shape of the trip before you book.

Browsing is free. There is no account to create. Holiday Chasers does not sell flights and does not take payment. When you want the seat, you book on the airline’s site, or on the partner search, as described in how it works.

Fares are scanned and refreshed every few days. They are a starting point. The price still has to be confirmed on the booking site.

Questions go to info@holidaychasers.ie.

Real fares from airline data · prices are indicative and confirmed at booking
