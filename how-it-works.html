Cork · Dublin · Ireland

How it works

Holiday Chasers finds cheap return fares from Cork and Dublin, the two biggest airports, for travellers anywhere in Ireland.

What the price is

The deal board lists return flights scanned from Cork Airport and Dublin Airport. Each price is for the flight only, per person. It does not include a hotel, a car, or bags and seats the airline adds later.

Weekend breaks and one-week trips are scanned automatically every few days. Inside a trip guide you can also check direct fares on dates around the ones you pick. A group total in that guide is the flight price multiplied by the number of travellers you selected. Hotels, cars and daily costs are shown separately.

Where you book

Holiday Chasers does not sell the seat and does not take payment. A book button leaves this site:

  • When the scanned airline is Ryanair, the button opens Ryanair’s own site with the route and dates filled in. You pay Ryanair.
  • When the scanned airline is Aer Lingus, the button opens Aer Lingus’s own site the same way. You pay Aer Lingus.
  • For any other airline, and for the “compare” search, the button opens Aviasales, the flight-search partner this site uses. The route and dates are filled in. You finish on Aviasales, or on the airline they send you to. Those links include a partner reference.

Hotel, car-hire and transfer buttons are separate. They open Booking.com, Airbnb, DiscoverCars, Rentalcars.com, Auto Europe or Welcome Pickups. They are not part of the flight price.

Prices can change

A fare on Holiday Chasers is a recently scanned price, not a held seat. Airlines change prices. The amount on the airline or partner site when you book is the one that counts. Confirm it before you pay.

What the site stores, and which services it contacts while you browse, is described on the privacy page.
