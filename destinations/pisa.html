Airport to the city

PisaMover, the shuttle operator, says it connects Galileo Galilei Airport with Pisa Centrale station, calling at an intermediate stop on the way. It runs every day from 6:00 to midnight, and until 1:00 from 1 June to 30 September. The operator says the ride between the airport and Pisa Centrale takes about 5 minutes, including that stop.
