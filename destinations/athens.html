Airport to the city

Athens International Airport says Metro Line 3, the blue line, connects the airport with Athens city centre and with Piraeus, at Dimotiko Theatro. The same page estimates 60 minutes by that metro to Piraeus station, and 65 minutes to Piraeus by suburban railway. The suburban railway also connects the airport with Larissis station. Express buses on the airport's page are X95 to Syntagma, X96 to Piraeus, X93 to the Kifissos and Liosion intercity bus stations, and X97 to Elliniko. The airport estimates 90 minutes on the X96 to Piraeus. It does not give a minute count for the metro into the centre, so none is stated here.
