Airport to the city

Metro do Porto's own FAQ puts the airport at Aeroporto, zone N10, and Trindade at zone C1. It says the trip from Aeroporto to Trindade crosses four zones, so the Andante title for that ride is a Z4. Andante is the card for the metro, for STCP buses and for CP urban trains. The FAQ does not give a journey time, so none is stated here.
