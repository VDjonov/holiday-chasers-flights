Airport to the city

Tenerife South Airport's own page lists Titsa buses. Line 40 runs Costa Adeje station, Los Cristianos and the airport. Line 10 runs Santa Cruz, Cruce Caletillas, Cruce San Isidro and the airport. Night line 711 runs Santa Cruz, the airport, Los Cristianos and Costa Adeje. Line 415 runs El Fraile, the airport and San Isidro. Line 343 runs direct between Tenerife Sur and Tenerife Norte with no stops, and the airport says that ride takes 50 minutes. This page does not copy the Titsa timetable.
